Nine things, one app

Each section works on its own and shares one look. Where it can, it works without internet.

🏠

Home

A dashboard with your counts, shortcuts and the things you used last.

🖼️

Wallpapers

Browse photos online or use built-in gradients offline. Save to your gallery or set as home or lock screen.

📺

Anime

Discover, search and track episodes and scores, powered by the free Jikan API. Your list works offline.

🎬

Movies

A watchlist, a watched history and your own star ratings, with search and sorting.

✨

AI Hub

Chat, rewrite, summarize, translate and brainstorm. Free public assistant, or bring your own provider. No keys are bundled.

🎮

Games

2048, Memory Match and Tic-Tac-Toe with an unbeatable mode. All offline, with saved records.

🎧

Music

Play songs stored on your phone with playlists, shuffle, repeat and lock-screen controls.

🧩

Widgets

A clock-and-stats widget and a four-button shortcut widget for your home screen.

🎨

Appearance

Dark, light or system theme, eight accent colours, pure-black mode and Material You colours.

Private by design

No ads, no trackers, no sign-up. Your lists, scores, playlists and settings are stored on your device. The app only goes online when you open a feature that needs it.

  • No advertising or analytics
  • No account required
  • Offline: games, gradients, movies, anime list, music
  • Your API key (optional) never leaves your phone

Install in a minute

  1. DownloadTap Download APK above on your Android phone.
  2. Open the fileOpen Glad.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. Allow the installIf asked, allow installs from your browser or file manager just this once.
  4. Say yes to accessGlad asks for music access only when you open Music, and notifications for playback controls.

Works on Android 8.0 or newer. Builds signed with the debug key are labelled debug-signed on the release page; they install normally, but you can only update them with another build signed the same way.
